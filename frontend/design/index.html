About Me

Engineer by training,
designer by drive.

I got into design through hackathons, and I got serious about it fast. Six national placements later, I had built a body of work that spoke for itself — full hi-fi prototypes, real problem statements, real competition.

Being a software engineer changes how I design. I know what a rerender costs. I understand how an API response shapes a screen. Every design decision I make accounts for how it will actually be built.

I graduated with a degree in Software Engineering from PUCIT, Lahore. I bring both sides of the product to every room I'm in.

Why I actually
love this.

01
🧩
Every screen is a puzzle
I love the constraint. You have limited space, a distracted user, and a business goal that needs to happen in three taps. That tension is exactly where good design lives.
02
🤝
The most honest feedback loop
You ship something and you immediately know if it worked. Users don't lie with their behavior. That directness is addictive once you've experienced it in a hackathon setting.
03
⚡
Engineering + design is a superpower
Most designers don't know what a rerender costs. Most engineers don't think about cognitive load. Being both means I never design something that can't be built — and I never build something that shouldn't exist.
